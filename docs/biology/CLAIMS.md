# Allowed claims

The model **may** claim:

- Recovery of MEG/PEG/biallelic calls for the reference panel against dosage nulls.
- Parent-of-origin (not accession) as the axis of conserved imprinting at `MEA`, `FWA`, `FIS2`, `PHE1`.
- Accession-variable `HDG3` imprinting via TE methylation, with induced-methylation restoration.
- Distinct DME versus ROS1 target sets and distinct maternal versus paternal Pol IV effects.
- Chalazal PEG enrichment and S-phase MEG reduction as coarse heterogeneity.
- ROS1-sensor disruption producing euchromatic loss with heterochromatic CHH recovery **in the homeostasis protocol only**.

The model **must not** claim:

- That Kubernetes pod death is cell death or a methylation stimulus (v0 metaphor is retired).
- That a DMR is causal for imprinting without an explicit expression rule.
- That arbitrary environmental stress creates adaptive transgenerational memory.
- That ROS1 homeostasis results are endosperm imprinting results.
- That v1 is a validated non-Arabidopsis simulator.
- That the PhenotypeAdapter is a biological mechanism.
